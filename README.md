# IN Network

**IN makes it easier to go together.**

IN is a trusted social network for turning ordinary plans into real-world connection.

The core problem is not discovery. People already know things they might want to do. The friction is execution: *Who is actually going? Do I know anyone? Will I be awkward? Where exactly do I go? Who is there when I arrive?*

IN bridges the gap between **"I'd go"** and **"I'm going."**

## Core product

An **IN** is something a person is already doing, whether planned weeks ahead or posted ten minutes before, that they are willing to let their trusted network join.

Examples:

- Barry's UES at 11, then Pura Vida after.
- Sheepshead Bay today. Bring a blanket, snacks and sunscreen.
- Yankees tonight. Billy's upstairs at 5.
- Central Park run Sunday morning, coffee after.
- MoMA after hours Thursday, dinner nearby afterward.
- Tennis at Riverside at 4.
- Rooftop drinks at 6.
- I'm making pasta at my apartment tonight.

IN does **not** require event-production behavior. A person should be able to type what they are doing in plain language and create an IN quickly.

## Product rules

1. **There is only "I'M IN."** No Maybe. No Decline. If someone does not want to join, they do nothing.
2. **People can see who is already IN before joining.** Positive social momentum matters.
3. **Joining unlocks the private IN chat.** No phone-number exchange is required.
4. **The chat is for hype + execution.** What should I bring? Where are you? I'm running late. We moved upstairs. I'm wearing a yellow shirt.
5. **The organizer can drop or update a location pin.** The pin is critical for real-world arrival and coordination.
6. **INs can be advanced or spontaneous.** Same system, different timing.
7. **The trust path is visible.** A participant can understand how they are connected to the organizer or other attendees.
8. **After the IN, members can choose whether to become directly IN with people they actually met.** Direct connection is mutual and deliberate.
9. **IN is socially agnostic.** Friendship, community, activity partners, professional friendships, couples, parents, students, doctors, newcomers and singles can all use the same system. Romance may happen, but it is not the product's purpose.
10. **The network grows through real-world interaction, not follower accumulation.**

## The trust graph

IN is not a follower graph. It is a graph of real-world social trust.

Example:

**Hannah → Marney → Grant → Alex**

Hannah may not know Alex, but she can see that Alex is connected through people she trusts. That makes joining materially less intimidating.

If Hannah attends Alex's IN, actually meets him, and both choose to stay directly IN afterward, the graph changes:

**Hannah ↔ Alex**

The platform becomes stronger because real-world participation creates new direct relationships.

## IN lifecycle

**CREATE → DISTRIBUTE → I'M IN → PRIVATE CHAT → PIN / ARRIVAL → REAL-WORLD EXPERIENCE → STAY IN**

### Create
The organizer types what they are doing in ordinary language.

### Distribute
IN exposes the plan to the appropriate trusted network based on connection distance and privacy choices.

### I'm IN
There is one positive commitment action: **I'M IN**.

### Private chat
Only people who are IN get access to the event's temporary group conversation.

### Pin / arrival
The organizer drops the event location pin and can update it as the group moves. Participants use chat to resolve final-foot arrival friction.

### Real-world experience
The product should help people get off the app and actually be together.

### Stay IN
Afterward, participants can privately decide whether they want to become directly IN with someone they met. Both parties must opt in.

## Ringleaders

Some members will naturally be high-execution organizers. Internally, IN can learn who consistently creates plans, shows up, coordinates people and brings groups together.

These people are **Ringleaders**.

They are not influencers. They are executors.

The product should never require every member to be one. Many members will mostly join, participate and expand their world through the plans others expose.

## What IN is not

IN is not:

- a dating app
- Eventbrite
- Meetup
- Facebook Events
- Instagram
- a follower network
- a "maybe interested" board
- a generic AI recommendation app
- a nightlife-only product

The system is built around **trusted participation and real-world execution**.

## Emotional north star

People want the feeling that **something is happening, they have a legitimate reason to be there, and somebody will recognize them when they arrive.**

IN makes strangers feel less like strangers by showing the social path between people and then helping them actually meet.

## Product thesis

> There is already more happening around you than you realize. IN gives you a trusted way into it.
