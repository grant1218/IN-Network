# IN product rules

IN is a trusted social network for real-world participation.

## Communication
- No person-to-person DMs.
- An actual IN unlocks a temporary group room for committed attendees.
- The room closes when the IN ends.
- Small-group rooms exist only to coordinate a shared activity and convert it into an IN.
- Direct IN relationships affect trust/distribution, never inbox access.

## Safety
- No dating/hookup/sexual solicitation as an IN, idea, profile intent, or chat purpose.
- Normal relationships can happen organically in real life; the platform does not facilitate romantic pursuit.
- Report/block controls are first-class.

## Core loop
IDEA → I'D BE IN → SMALL GROUP → MAKE IT AN IN → I'M IN → TEMPORARY ROOM → PIN → REAL WORLD → ROOM CLOSES → FUTURE IN
